About Me

I'm a software engineer who specializes in building scalable systems and automating complex workflows. I work across the full stack—from React frontends to Python backends, with a strong focus on cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP) and DevOps practices. My experience spans everything from modernizing legacy systems to deploying machine learning models in production.

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to work with industry leaders like Nvidia, Google, Apple, Microsoft, and Tesla, solving challenging problems in manufacturing, data analytics, and enterprise software. I thrive in Agile environments and have led teams as Scrum Master, where I've learned that the best solutions come from collaboration, clear communication, and a relentless focus on delivering value.

Currently pursuing my M.S. in Software Engineering with a focus on AI, while working full-time at TSMC. When I'm not coding or optimizing systems, you'll find me on the soccer field coaching club teams—applying the same principles of teamwork and continuous improvement that drive my engineering work.
